## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat). I read this dimension as asking whether the design, as written before any result exists, can answer the question it poses. For a pre-results plan with a draft pre-registered decision rule, that means: each candidate is pinned down well enough that someone else could run exactly it; each candidate is paired with a control that removes only the factor under test and keeps data, seeds, budget, preprocessing and evaluation identical; each bar states its metric, its comparator, its threshold and direction, and the noise level it was set against, and all of this is fixed before the runs; the kill and carry rules turn every possible outcome pattern into one action with no room for choice after the numbers arrive; the confirmatory fresh-seed test is separated from development and selection so that nothing learned while choosing candidates or setting bars can leak into it; evaluation data are disjoint from training and selection data, including where different records share an underlying source item; uncertainty and multiplicity are handled for every headline comparison; and the planned runs fit the stated time window. Results that do not exist yet are expected and are not held against the plan. What counts against it is any flaw that would leave a candidate's eventual result uninterpretable or would weaken the test below what it claims to show. Under the contract, a fatal finding here leads to rejection, a block or a warn each lead to major revision, and acceptance needs a pass here and on every other dimension.

D2 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by the editor-in-chief seat). This dimension asks whether the manuscript is organised and written clearly enough for a method-focused venue, and whether it follows that venue's conventions. From the methodology side it matters where exposition carries rigor: the decision rule, the definitions of each candidate, control and metric, and the run procedure should each be stated once, unambiguously and with consistent terms across the main text and any appended material, so that a reader could apply the rule mechanically. I do not score this dimension; under the contract, a warn or worse on it leads to minor revision.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Whether each candidate is a frozen, runnable configuration; whether each has a matched control that changes only the manipulated factor while sharing data, seeds, budget, preprocessing and evaluation; whether every bar names its metric, comparator, threshold, direction and the seed-noise estimate it was calibrated against, all fixed before results; whether the kill and carry rules are mechanical and map every outcome pattern to exactly one action; whether the fresh-seed confirmatory test uses seeds, episodes and splits never touched while developing or selecting candidates or setting bars; whether evaluation items are disjoint from training and selection items at the level of shared source items; whether multiple candidates and metrics are handled by a correction or a single declared primary comparison; whether uncertainty (seed spread, intervals or paired tests) is planned for every headline comparison; whether configs, seeds, commands and data versions are specified well enough to rerun each candidate; and whether the run budget fits the stated time window with a stated fallback order.
what_triggers_block: At least one candidate that the decision rule can carry forward would be uninterpretable as planned, because its declared matched control differs from it in more than the manipulated factor (such as preprocessing, normalisation, capacity, training budget, seeds or data), or its bar lacks a stated comparator, threshold, direction or noise calibration fixed before results, or the kill and carry rules leave some outcome pattern unassigned or let the action be chosen after seeing results, or an information path local to that candidate (a feature, grouping or label source computed with access to evaluation items) contaminates its evaluation.
what_triggers_warn: Every candidate remains interpretable, but the test is weakened by at least one of the following: a bar calibrated on a single seed or with no reported seed spread, several candidates or metrics tested with neither a multiplicity correction nor one declared primary comparison, a fresh-seed test with too few seeds to separate the bar from seed noise, reproducibility gaps (configs, seeds, commands, data versions or compute) that would stop an outside researcher from rerunning a candidate, or a run budget that does not fit the stated time window and has no stated fallback order.
what_triggers_fatal: The primary confirmatory test is structurally unable to discriminate the plan's main claim, because the fresh-seed or held-out evaluation reuses data, seeds, episodes or source items already used to develop, tune or select candidates or to set the bars, or because the main claim is compared against no baseline or control at all, so that no outcome of the planned runs could falsify it without redesigning the study.
