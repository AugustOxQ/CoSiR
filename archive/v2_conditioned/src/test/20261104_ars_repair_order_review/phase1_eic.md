## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat). From an editorial oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the work's experimental logic would survive scrutiny by a methods referee: how the studies are designed, how data are partitioned and handled, how statistics are reported, and whether someone else could reproduce the procedure. Because the contract frames the document as a decision memo written before results exist, the question shifts from "are the results sound" to "would the planned steps, run in the planned order under the rules fixed in advance, actually tell apart the explanations each step claims to separate, within the available time, while leaving untouched the evaluation material reserved for the later go/no-go test". Results that do not exist yet are expected and are not grounds for a block on their own; a design that leaves a step unreadable, or that erodes the later go/no-go test, is. This seat does not score D1. I note only that its failure conditions carry the most editorial weight: a fatal block from any reviewer means rejection, and any block or warn means a major revision.

D2 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by this seat). This dimension asks whether the document is organised and written so that a reader can find and follow its argument, and whether it follows the usual conventions for a method-focused write-up. For a decision memo with supporting material appended, good structure means that the decision being made, the options considered, the chosen order and the rule that governs each step can be found quickly and read unambiguously, and that the appended evidence supports the memo rather than burying it. A warn or worse here from any reviewer leads to a minor revision. Acceptance needs every dimension to pass. No target-criteria binding was supplied, so I will judge conventions only against general norms for method-focused decision documents and will make no claim about fit to any specific venue.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether the memo states its decision question, the candidate orderings, the recommended order and each step's pre-stated decision rule in one locatable place ahead of the appended evidence; whether every outcome-to-action mapping, time budget and data-reservation commitment is stated once and consistently across memo body and appendices; whether terms are defined at first use and named consistently throughout; whether memo claims point to the appended section they rest on; and whether the write-up follows general conventions for method-focused decision documents (rules stated before results, explicit scope, reproducibility pointers), with no claim about any specific venue.
what_triggers_block: The recommended order, or at least one step's decision rule, cannot be located, is stated in conflicting forms between the memo body and the appended material, or is so entangled with the appended evidence that a reader cannot determine which commitments the memo actually makes, so the document cannot function as a fixed-in-advance decision record.
what_triggers_warn: The commitments can be recovered but only with effort: terms left undefined at first use or renamed between sections, outcome-to-action mappings scattered across several places, memo claims without pointers to the appended evidence they rely on, an unclear boundary between memo and appendices, or redundancy that obscures the decision path; the absence of results alone never triggers this.
