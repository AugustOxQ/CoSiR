## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor; mandatory; owned by and eligible only for the methodology seat). I read this dimension as asking whether the study design, data handling, statistical reporting and reproducibility affordances would satisfy a methodology referee in this field. The stage note makes the object of judgement a plan rather than finished results: the manuscript is a decision memo written before results, proposing an order of method repair steps, with supporting evidence appended. So I judge whether each proposed step has a design that can separate the competing readings it says it tests, with a decision rule fixed in advance (statistic, comparison arm, threshold, number of seeds or draws, uncertainty estimate); whether the proposed order follows from what each earlier outcome would change later; whether the whole sequence fits the stated time window; whether the plan keeps the fresh test episodes and held rows untouched so that the later GO test stays an unbiased, single look; and whether a third party could rerun each step from what is written. Absent results are expected and do not count against the memo. A design flaw that would leave a step uninterpretable, or that would weaken the later GO test, does count. Under the failure conditions, a fatal block on D1 ends in rejection, and a block or a warn on D1 each ends in major revision, so D1 alone can prevent acceptance.

D2 (writing_and_structure; normal priority; owned by and eligible only for the EIC seat). This dimension covers how the manuscript is organised, how clearly it explains itself, and whether it follows the conventions of a method focused paper at the venue. It is not eligible for my role, so I make no scoring plan for it and give it no score. Its only point of contact with my work is that exposition can change methodological meaning: where an ambiguity in wording makes a design or a decision rule admit two readings, I treat the resulting rigor problem under D1, and I leave the quality of prose and layout to the EIC. A warn or worse on D2 would by itself lead to minor revision, and acceptance requires every dimension to pass.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: For every proposed repair step and for the order as a whole: the hypothesis and the rival readings the step claims to separate; a decision rule fixed before any result (statistic, baseline or control arm, numeric threshold, seeds or draws, uncertainty estimate); a reason to expect the predicted outcomes under the rival readings to differ by more than seed or bootstrap noise (power, minimum detectable effect, or prior spread); an argument that each earlier outcome changes what a later step does, so the order matters; a data use ledger stating which splits each step touches and showing that the fresh test episodes and held rows reserved for the later GO test are never used for selection, tuning, ordering or early looks; a per step compute and time budget that fits the window with a stated fallback; and rerun affordances (configs, seeds, commands, data and index hashes, code versions); missing results are not scored.
what_triggers_block: At least one proposed step could not discriminate the readings it claims to test as designed (no decision rule, a rule fixed after seeing data, a threshold that seed or bootstrap noise alone could cross, or every plausible outcome leading to the same next action), or the order rests on an unstated or wrong dependency, or the plan would weaken the later GO test (it touches the reserved test episodes or held rows, allows repeated looks at them, or lets the GO criterion be revised after repair results), or the sequence cannot reach the GO test within the window; in each case the defect is still fixable by redesign before anything is run.
what_triggers_warn: Every step is interpretable and the reserved evaluation data stay protected, but rigor gaps lower confidence without making a step uninterpretable: thresholds or seed and draw counts given without a noise or power justification, a missing baseline or control arm for a secondary claim, several outcomes or forking analysis choices across steps without a named primary outcome or a correction, a time budget with no slack or fallback, or incomplete rerun details (seeds, configs, hashes, commands).
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's own record shows that the fresh test episodes or held rows reserved for the GO test have already been consulted, tuned on or otherwise spent, or the plan as a whole contains no step able to discriminate any of the readings it names, so that no reordering or redesign could restore either an unbiased GO test or a usable basis for the decision the memo exists to make.
