- Claims are pooled over three aspect pairs: emotion with style, emotion with genre, and style with genre.

## How we got here

CoSiR tests image and text similarity on frozen features. Example pairs show which aspect counts.

A grouping partitions training rows without labels. The reader is round 1's model: it reads example pairs and says which grouping they share. B′(A0) is the condition-free scorer built on the reader's three groupings, the main bar of the test.

Condition-free scores do not change when the condition is swapped, so their condition gain is zero. R@1 measures how often the target ranks strictly first. A held read tests the final test paintings once per method and backbone.

In round 6, AFF won the CLIP held read, +0.60 R@1 points over B′(A0). Round 9 tested two more backbones and translated captions. It used the same recipe, re-fitted on each backbone's features, with no new choices.

## Two more backbones

**Problem.** Transfer beyond CLIP was open.

**Idea.** We re-fitted AFF on Qwen and SigLIP 2. AFF adds the reader's term only when the reader picks the affect grouping.

The matched control removes only the condition from the same scorer. RCA, relevant component analysis, reweights features from example pairs. B is the earlier condition-free scorer.

The seven checks require AFF to beat cosine, RCA, B, B′(A0), and its matched control on R@1. They also require condition gain over the matched control and RCA.

**Did it work?** Yes, both passed all seven checks. An episode is one ranking problem with example pairs and 13 candidates. Earlier rounds read these paintings; both reused the episodes of the round 6 read. SigLIP 2 also depends on the refit below.

## The language check

**Problem.** Transfer to translated captions was open.

**Idea.** We tested ArtELingo-28 on all three backbones; AFF stayed trained on English ArtELingo. Captions came from 27 other languages, machine-translated to English. The two-level test has:

- Level 1: beat B′(A0) on R@1 and keep a condition gain over the matched control.
- Level 2: beat cosine, RCA, B, and the matched control on R@1; keep a condition gain over RCA.

Level 2 is tested only when both level 1 checks pass. Level 1 alone permits a small claim; both levels together permit the full claim.

## Glossary

- **AFF:** the reader's term (R1), added to the score only when the reader picks the affect grouping.
- **B:** the earlier condition-free scorer.
- **B′(A0):** B rebuilt on three groupings; the main bar.
- **B′(A1):** B′(A0) plus a style grouping; the strongest condition-free scorer.
- **Matched control (CF):** the same scorer with only the condition removed.
- **RCA:** relevant component analysis; reweights features from example pairs.
- **Condition gain:** correct minus swapped R@1 for the same target.
- **R@1:** share of targets ranked strictly first, in percentage points.
- **Held read:** final test, once per method and backbone.
- **ArtELingo-28:** the translated-caption language check.
- **Full claim:** all seven checks pass.
- **Small claim:** level 1 alone passes.
- **Inconclusive:** the test cannot tell the edge from zero.
