Help

Each map is the attention of the pooled last token (the one that becomes the embedding) on the image tokens: raw attention, averaged over heads, summed over the chosen layers, then rescaled so the map over the image sums to 1.

The first token takes most of the attention (attention sink), so the bar under each panel shows how the raw attention is split (first token, instruction, image, caption, other template tokens); the map only shows the image part.

In image + text, click a caption word to see that word's attention over the image. Click it again or press Escape to go back. Left and right arrows change the painting.
