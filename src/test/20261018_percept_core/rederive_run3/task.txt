## Data

- subset_pid (5,000 painting indices): the evaluation paintings. The order of subset_pid is the row order of every
  5,000-row output below.

Fit rows: /tmp/claude-0/-project-CoSiR/724fc430-8911-4bb5-ba46-fe27b9e6c38a/scratchpad/rederive3/fit_rows.npy, int64,
the caption row indices (into the 99,999 captions, ascending) of the 13,400 fit paintings: the training pairs.

Labels of the 5,000 evaluation paintings, in subset_pid order:

- emotion: the majority of emo over the painting's captions with emo >= 0 (9 classes, 0 to 8); ties go to the smallest
  class id; -1 when no caption of the painting has emo >= 0.
- style: sty of the painting's first caption (row first[p]).
- genre: gen of the painting's first caption.
- Each label is scored only on paintings with label >= 0.

The clustering score of a 5,000 x d matrix X against a label vector y:

1. X as float32.
2. For r in 0, 1, 2: pred_r = sklearn.cluster.MiniBatchKMeans(n_clusters=20, random_state=r, n_init=3,
   batch_size=4096).fit_predict(X).
3. ami_r = sklearn.metrics.adjusted_mutual_info_score(y[y >= 0], pred_r[y >= 0]), default arguments.
4. score = mean of ami_r over r (also keep the population sd, np.std with ddof=0).

The same three partitions serve all three labels of one matrix.

## Part A: the Stage 2 outputs (the image side)

Folder: /project/CoSiR/src/test/20261018_percept_core/outputs/stage2/. Read only the *.npz files whose name has the
form <arm>__s<seed>.npz, arm in P1, P2, P3, P4, P5 and seed in 0, 1, 2 (skip any name ending in .pending.npz). Each
holds one array, eval_prob: 5,000 x K float32, the sigmoid output of one trained model for the 5,000 evaluation
paintings, rows in subset_pid order, values in [0, 1]. All 15 files should exist and every file should have K = 40
columns; list any absent file or any other K as a problem.

For each file:

1. Check the shape (5,000 rows, 40 columns), that every value is finite and in [0, 1].
2. Image-side score: l2-normalize each row of eval_prob (x / max(norm, 1e-12)), then the clustering score above
   against each of the three labels.
3. Argmax score: topic = argmax of eval_prob per row (first maximum on ties); AMI of topic against each label
   (adjusted_mutual_info_score, default arguments, on paintings with label >= 0; no k-means).
4. Record K.

Per arm, over the seeds present: the mean and the population sd (ddof=0) of the image-side score per label, and of
the argmax score per label.

**The comparator** per aspect (aspect = emotion, style, genre; the label equal to the aspect), computed twice:

- (a) over the established arms. Established arms (a status given to you, not a number): P1, P2, P3, P4, P5 (all five arms are established in this run).
- (b) over all arms with all three seeds present.

In each case: the arm with the highest mean image-side score on that label. If other arms in the set are within 0.003
of that highest mean (highest minus theirs <= 0.003), the comparator is the one with the lowest arm number among them
(P1 < P2 < ... < P5). Report the comparator arm, its mean and sd, and the highest arm and its mean, per aspect and per
case.

## Part B: the Stage 1 outputs (the kept topics)

Folder: /project/CoSiR/src/test/20261018_percept_core/outputs/stage1/. Read only the 9 files <version>__s<seed>.npz,
version in clip_roberta, qwen_qwen, qwen_roberta and seed in 0, 1, 2. Each holds:

- center_norms (100,) float: the norms of the 100 trained topic centers;
- fit_counts (100,) int64: per center, how many fit pairs have it as their argmax center over all 100 centers;
- kept (100,) bool: the kept centers;
- topic (99,999,) integer: per caption (all captions, not only fit), its topic index among the kept centers taken in
  ascending center order (0 to 39);
- q_paint_eval (5,000 x 40) float: per evaluation painting (subset_pid order), the mean topic weights of its captions
  over the kept centers.

The rule for the kept centers: the 40 centers with the largest fit_counts; on equal counts the lower center index is
kept. fit_counts and topic come from two separate passes of the model, so a fit pair that lies near a tie between two
centers can land differently in each. Do not rebuild fit_counts from topic, and do not treat a small difference
between them as an error.

For each file:

1. Check: shapes and dtypes as listed; kept.sum() == 40; fit_counts.sum() equals the number of fit rows; kept equals
   your own top-40 pick from fit_counts by the rule above; every topic value in 0 to 39; q_paint_eval finite.
2. Coverage: with_fit_pair = how many of the 40 topics hold at least one fit pair in topic[fit_rows]; with_1pct = how
   many of the 40 hold at least 1% of the fit pairs (count * 100 >= number of fit rows); share_before_cut =
   fit_counts[kept].sum() / number of fit rows.
3. The 40th and 41st largest fit_counts (the gap at the cut).
4. Stage 1 argmax score: topic = argmax of q_paint_eval per row (first maximum on ties); AMI against each label
   (default arguments, on paintings with label >= 0).

Per version: the mean and population sd over the 3 seeds of each Stage 1 argmax AMI.
