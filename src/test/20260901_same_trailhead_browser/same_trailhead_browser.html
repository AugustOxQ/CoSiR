What “pull” means here

Every sample gets a trainable condition vector, initialized from this buddy graph. Pull measures how much closer two vectors sit than a degree-matched random pair. Across the four real C–D connectivity buckets, it rises monotonically: no trail < text trail < image trail < confirmed route.
