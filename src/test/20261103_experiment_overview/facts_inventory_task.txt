## Context

CoSiR builds c*, a condition-free model over a frozen image encoder: one shared trunk with emotion, style and genre
heads, trained with "buddy" graph losses built from Qwen3-VL-Embedding instructed image views, plus a teacher term
(the "teacher" is the instructed Qwen image of an aspect). The main task is clustering (k-means with 20 clusters, AMI
against ArtELingo-style labels); the secondary task is label retrieval (query-by-example, mAP). The "new line" started
2026-10-15.
