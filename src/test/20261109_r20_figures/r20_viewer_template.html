CoSiR candidate qualitative figures (review page, not the paper figure)

Every candidate design is computed by fixed rules, so you can compare them before choosing. c* is our model, seed 0 (one head per aspect); Plain Qwen image is the frozen Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B image without instruction. Clusters are k-means (r17 code, state 0) on all paintings; only public-domain paintings are shown (artist died 1955 or earlier, per Wikidata). Cluster rules: per class (for each class, the cluster with the largest share of that class), purity (6 purest clusters with at least 20 labelled members), random (6 clusters drawn with seed 0 among clusters with at least 20 members). Painting rules: centre (closest to the cluster centre) or random (seed 0); near copies (plain cosine 0.95 or more) are skipped. Shares ignore unlabelled paintings.
