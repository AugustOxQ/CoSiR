- **Photo-model emotion embeddings exist**, for seeds 0–4: `/data/SSD2/cosir_r14/20261102_backbones_sweep/outputs/exports/affection/photo_siglip1/test__s{0..4}.npz`. The sheet uses `test__s0.npz`, whose `emb` array is 8,501 × 2,048, float16, and whose `keys` identify the Affection test photos. Its adjacent `test__s0.json` fields `dataset`, `arm`, `split`, `seed`, `rows`, and `checkpoint` identify Affection, `photo_siglip1`, test, seed 0, 8,501 rows, and `/data/SSD2/cosir_r14/20261102_backbones_sweep/outputs/photo/siglip1/s0/emotion.pt`.
- The sidecar's `width` is 1,152, **the input width**, not the export dimension: `src/test/20261102_backbones_sweep/bs_photo.py:464` passes the input `width` into inference and writes it into the sidecar; the actual `emb.shape` is 8,501 × 2,048.
- The checkpoint's `run.json` has `encoder: siglip1`, `row: ref`, `settings.start: cstar`, `cstar_seed: 0`, and `weights_sha256` matching the export sidecar's `checkpoint_sha256`. `src/test/20261102_backbones_sweep/bs_photo.py:1` documents the Affection photo fine-tune from the corresponding painting c* checkpoint. This is the photo model, not the r28 painting-only export. The paper's `Tables/photos.tex:15` records the SigLIP row's 0.182 AMI and 0.323 mAP; the sheet is one seed, not the aggregate.
- **Plain features exist:** `/data/SSD2/pre_extract/affection_local/siglip_so400m/img.npy`, indexed by `keys.json`. `meta.json` fields `dataset`, `view`, `model_id`, and `shape` identify Affection, siglip1, `google/siglip-so400m-patch14-384`, and 85,007 × 1,152 (all splits); `img.npy` is float16. The test subset has 8,501 rows after the key join.
- **IDs match:** `make_candidates.py` checks uniqueness, exact photo-export versus annotation test-key sets, membership in the plain store, and equality of both ordered ID lists after joining to the sorted annotation keys, for both queries and gallery. The native stores have different orders; their rows are not joined by position. Results are in `results.json`.
- **Candidate sheet:** [contact_sheet.png](contact_sheet.png), 1,809 × 2,136 pixels at 150 dpi. [candidates.csv](candidates.csv) has one row per displayed image, source dataset and original image filename from the Affection annotation fields `dataset` and `image_name`; the metadata supplies no original URL. Source identifiers do not establish licence status.
- [make_candidates.py](make_candidates.py) uses cosine, excludes the query, and skips tied plurality labels from both queries and gallery. The annotation CSV's `emotion` votes and `split` determine labels; ties are identified using `ac_data.ties`. Of 8,501 test photos, 1,917 have tied plurality and 6,584 are eligible (`results.json`).
- The rule gives 186 qualifying queries (`results.json`): CoSiR matches ≥4/5 and plain matches ≤1/5. Rank by match-count gain descending then key, with at most two per emotion, as the applicable part of panel (a)'s `qf_cand.py:select_a`; genre, painting public-domain and near-copy rules are not applied to photos. The first 12 are shown, with 129 unique photos. Check marks indicate matching labels.
