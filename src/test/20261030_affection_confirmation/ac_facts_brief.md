## Datasets and labels

**Affection.** Human emotion choices with visual explanations for real-world photos, using the ArtEmis annotation protocol; **85,007 images, 526,749 explanations**, at least **6 annotators/image** according to the literature note. Sources: COCO **22,766**, Visual Genome **16,634**, Flickr30k **13,196**, Emotional Machines **10,603**, and You et al./FI **21,808** images. These source counts sum to the release total; cross-source visual deduplication is **not checked**. [LIT:14–21,36; DL:21,25–35,45,52]

Emotion is attached to each explanation, not a canonical image class. The **9** names are amusement, anger, awe, contentment, disgust, excitement, fear, sadness, and `something else`. No style or genre labels are provided by the inspected adapter/store; a source-dataset label is provenance, not either of those aspects. Explanations are present; no separate explanatory class exists. [LIT:36,53–62; AD:296–297,322–334; A/index.json:texts[*].emotion, images[*].source]

The release's image-level majority/tie convention is **not checked**. The existing CoSiR rule is **plurality**, with ties to the lowest alphabetically ordered emotion code; it does not require an absolute majority. The adapter currently preserves individual annotation labels and does not apply this rule. [src/test/20261027_recipe_sweep/rs_vh.py:171–180; AD:331–334]

**Official split:** paper reports **85/5/10 train/val/test**, image-disjoint. Local annotation counts are train **443,258**, val **26,113**, test **52,188**, ignored **5,190**; **5,035 images** mix ignored with one scored split, but none mixes train, val and test. These annotation proportions are not the image proportions. [LIT:59; DL:23–24]

**Impressions (Kruk et al., EMNLP 2023).** Images with aesthetic, impression and descriptive/caption texts, news-or-web provenance, and human Impact scores on a **1–4** scale. The inspected logs do not establish exact source sites or their shares: **not checked**. Current store: **1,442 unique image files**, including **1,015 headline-overlay versions**, representing **822 UUIDs**; UUIDs identify base-photo families, not proven pixel identities. [DL:6–15; I/index.json:extra.n_images, extra.n_overlay_images; computed distinct images[*].uuid]

There are **no emotion, artistic-style or genre classes** in the logged release. `caption_type` labels text rows, not images: counts aesthetic **4,275**, description **4,271**, impression **4,272**, caption **2,129**, computed over **14,947 texts**. An image can have several caption types, so these counts are not image-class shares. Impact is saved as a per-ImgId mean, observed range **1.0–3.555555555555556**, with **0 missing**; no categorical emotion label can be read from it. Emotion/style/genre class shares and their label AMI/mAP are therefore undefined with the supplied labels. [DL:13–15; AD:253–284; I/index.json:extra.n_impact_missing, images[*].impact_mean, texts[*].caption_type]

The adapter reads official named **train/test annotation files**, no validation file. Train texts **12,123**, expanded test texts **2,824**. Image memberships: train-only **736**, test-only **34**, both **672**; thus **1,408** train and **706** test files. The official annotation split is not an image-disjoint split. UUID-level cross-split overlap and a separate official validation split are **not checked**. [AD:259–284; I/index.json:extra.n_train_texts, extra.n_test_texts; computed images[*].splits]

**c* export:** frozen default Qwen images pass through the saved image trunk/head for each aspect and seed; no new caption features, graphs or teacher views enter that forward pass. Weights are under `RT/rt_data.py:CSTAR_ROOT`, `weights/k11=40/{aspect}__s{seed}.pt`. RT explicitly replays the heads on **CPU**, so this export need not be a GPU step. Output is unit-normalized float16, width **2,048**. New-set outputs are not among A/I's built stores. [RT/rt_stage0.py:204–215; RB/rb_student.py:300–319; RT/rt_data.py:22,149–172]

**PercepT core export:** use the frozen full-scale **P3, K=40** mapper for seeds **0–4**, producing float32 sigmoid topic probabilities of width **40** from default Qwen image rows. Existing code routes through ArtELingo-specific image loaders and enforces the training CUDA device; new identities/input arrays need a loader path. R11's final amendment uses full-scale mapper weights for both validation and held, with weight hashes checked. [CT/ct_percept.py:530–567; RT/rt_percept_val.py:44–71; RT/rt_data.py:200–220]

**Our CRL:** normalized CLIP L/14 image vectors, width **768**, are compared with CLIP text vectors for **50** generated values/aspect; their cosine-to-value vectors are the aspect embeddings. RT computes these products and normalization in float64, then casts to float32. The existing LLM request and value-text template describe **paintings**. Whether r12 reuses those texts or changes the domain wording is **not checked**; either case still requires new-set L/14 images and aligned CRL vectors. [RT/rt_crl.py:18–24,82–100,123–144; RT/rt_data.py:232–237,253–259; RT/20261029_retrieval_log.md:12]

## Protocol inheritance and limits

- **r10 clustering:** image-head AMI using MiniBatchKMeans **k=20**, states **0,1,2**, `n_init=3`, batch **4,096**, float32 unit rows. Model seeds **0–4**; paired **95%** painting/seed bootstrap with **2,000** draws over fixed partitions. PercepT also has its own argmax reading. [R10:65–83; src/test/20261027_recipe_sweep/rs_vh.py:239–254; RB/rb_score.py:63–73,88–104]
- **r10 scales/bar:** sample training **13,400 fit + 1,489 stopping** paintings versus full **42,969 train + 6,152 validation**, both evaluated on **12,281 held** paintings. G's held emotion bar was mean margin **≥0.0100**, lower bound **>0**, against the strongest eligible PercepT entry, with full GO and sample not NO. c*'s held teacher outcomes used lower bound **>0** for WIN, otherwise **>−r** for LEVEL; its PercepT emotion margin also had the **0.0100** bar. Frozen full-scale c* transfer does not create a new sample/full training comparison. [R10:63–68,89–107; src/test/20261027_recipe_sweep/DECISION_RULE.md:46–54]
- **Clustering r is ArtELingo-specific:** **0.0046 / 0.0082 / 0.0128** for emotion/style/genre, defined as D2 AMI max−min over model seeds **0,1,2** on the old **5,000** development paintings. These are not measured Affection/Impressions variability. [GLOSSARY.md:15]
- **r11 retrieval:** image query plus aspect, same labelled images as gallery with self excluded; same-label relevance, full-ranking AP averaged equally over queries, descriptive P@**10**, stable gallery-order ties. Seeded arms average query metrics over seeds **0–4**. Joint query/seed bootstrap: **10,000** draws, **95%** percentile intervals, fixed gallery. These metric definitions can be applied where image labels exist. [SPEC11:113–136]
- **r11 bar/ranges:** WIN over plain Qwen and LEVEL/WIN against instructed Qwen on every labelled aspect; WIN lower bound **>0**, LEVEL lower bound **>−r**. Retrieval r is c* validation max−min over seeds **0–4**, plain cosine: **0.0014 / 0.0022 / 0.0038** rounded. It differs from clustering r. R11's **6,152** validation paintings selected each arm's read-out; stage **5** carries its claim, stage **1** is untuned. [R11:59–86,131–143; SPEC11:29–33,131–133]
- The selected read-outs were centering plus query expansion **k=10** for c*, plain/instructed Qwen and PercepT; CRL used no centering plus **k=10**. Selection used cross-fitting on ArtELingo validation; held transforms fit on validation, not held. Neither these picks nor their fit statistics are a new-data validation result. [R11:131–143,196; SPEC11:22–23; RT/rt_data.py:33–49]
- Affection's official val split could supply separate labelled validation images; the current store has **3,129** such images under the membership rule above, with the full FI-inclusive count **not checked**. Impressions supplies no validation set in the inspected adapter and has shared train/test files. No r12 validation policy, new-dataset margin values, or numeric CRL bar was verified. CRL having a bar is decided, but r11's descriptive status does not supply its threshold. [A/index.json:images[*].splits; AD:259; DIR:71–76]
- No style/genre labels means no corresponding label AMI, label mAP or full three-column aspect-switch claim on these releases. Affection's annotation distributions permit the r11 graded-emotion overlap definition; Impressions lacks the emotion distributions it requires. [DL:13–15,30; SPEC11:120–136]
- **ArtELingo-28 “qualified”:** **A-QUAL** meant emotion AMI margin **d>0**, but lower confidence bound **≤0**; A-HOLDS required positive margin and lower bound, A-FAILS meant **d≤0**. The margin was student k-means AMI minus the strongest included PercepT entry/reading, not minus the instructed teacher; style/genre and culture subsets were descriptive. On **746** paintings the two Qwen recipes and SigLIP 2 were qualified; CLIP B/32 failed. These were earlier recipes, not a c* transfer result. [RB/DECISION_RULE.md:237–244; RB/rb_a28.py:120–129,238–254; RB/rb_score.py:69–73; DIR:234–245; R10:70]

## Risks and verification gaps

- **Overlap:** Affection's declared photo sources differ from ArtELingo's WikiArt paintings, but exact duplicates, derived images and pretraining exposure were **not checked**. Impressions–ArtELingo/WikiArt overlap is also **not checked**. ArtELingo-28 is explicitly an ArtELingo/WikiArt subset, so its earlier transfer result was not evidence for unseen image content. [LIT:15–19,27–28,74–81]
- **Noise/imbalance:** literature reports Affection **71.3% positive / 21.1% negative / 7.6% other annotation choices**, **50.0%** of images receiving both positive and negative labels, and **67.5% (57,381 images)** having a strong-majority emotion. Those release-wide facts differ from the current-store plurality counts and tie counts above. Emotion words and visual references in explanations are intentional; their rates were **not checked**. [LIT:53–62]
- **Impressions dependence:** headline variants and shared images make annotation rows dependent. Earlier train-only analysis found **814 source photos** behind **12,123 records**; the current combined store has **822 UUIDs**, a different scope. Caption-type labels cannot become one image label by simply repeating image rows. [docs/reports/auto/buddy/2026-06-22_buddy_analysis.md:18–33; I/index.json:images,texts; computed counts above]
- **Rights:** Affection language data came through the user's request/download; exact Affection terms were not summarized in the literature note and are **not checked** here. Source-image rights remain with their sources; the paper licence is not an image licence. Emotional Machines' figshare release is logged as **CC BY 4.0**. T4SA's MIT licence covers code; image terms are unstated there, and FI research-use terms are **not checked**. Impressions licence/terms are **not checked** in the inspected sources. [DL:19–20,31–34,45–46; LIT:36,122]
- **Removal list:** no deletion was performed and no additional removal candidate was established by this facts inspection. DL:62 already records removal of the Emotional Machines tarball and FI `.git` with prior approval; their former sizes in DL:47–48 are historical, not current removal candidates. The legacy CLIP store and debug image listed above remain present.
