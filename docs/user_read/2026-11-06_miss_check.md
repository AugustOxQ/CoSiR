# MMArt's failed counts follow its text, not its paintings or labels

> 2026-11-06 · Full report: [short check of the MMArt miss](../reports/auto/percept/2027-01-12_miss_check.md)

## Summary

- The last test failed on MMArt, a second painting set, and we did not know why.
- We reran the test on paintings that are also in ArtELingo, where the same test passed.
- With MMArt's text, these paintings get the same win counts as MMArt's new paintings.
- With ArtELingo's captions, c* gets its ArtELingo count back. A label swap changes no count.
- So the counts follow the text. We do not yet know which property of the text matters.
- The one cell below plain Qwen comes from two large classes, portrait and landscape.

## How we got here

c* is our model. It gives emotion, style and genre spaces, and it learned its text side on ArtELingo's short human captions.
The last test scored c* on two sets: ArtELingo, where it held, and MMArt, where it failed.
MMArt's text is long and generated by a Qwen model. This check asks where the failure comes from.

## Check 1: which genres hold the miss

**Problem.** c* missed plain Qwen in one cell: genre, with text as both query and gallery. A cell is one aspect in one direction, such as genre from text to image, and each test has 9 cells.

**Idea.** Split the gap by genre class. A class's contribution is its share of queries times its difference, and the contributions sum to the gap.

**Did it work.** Yes. Portrait's contribution alone equals the whole gap. Landscape and abstract also pull down, and the 7 classes above plain Qwen cancel about that much.

**Key evidence.** c* minus plain Qwen. The full report has the 10 classes.

| Class | Share of queries | Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait | 23% | -0.06 |
| Landscape | 19% | -0.05 |
| Abstract | 9% | -0.03 |

The gap stays when we remove texts that name their own genre, so explicit label words do not explain it.

## Check 2: paintings, labels or text

**Problem.** MMArt differs from ArtELingo in three ways: its new paintings, its text and its labels. The last test could not separate them.

**Idea.** Use the 11,528 paintings that are in both sets, and cross the two texts with the two labels. New paintings then drop out as a cause, and the text and labels each change alone.

**Did it work.** Yes. The win counts follow the text. This holds for MMArt's narrative text, the only MMArt text we ran here. It does not say which property of that text matters: author, length or the number of texts per painting.

**Key evidence.** Cells won of 9, against the teacher's text.

| Paintings | Text | Labels | c* | Late fusion (c* joined with plain Qwen) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shared | ArtELingo captions | ArtELingo | 6 | 4 |
| Shared | ArtELingo captions | MMArt | 6 | 4 |
| Shared | MMArt narrative | ArtELingo | 3 | 0 |
| Shared | MMArt narrative | MMArt | 3 | 0 |
| New | MMArt narrative | MMArt | 3 | 0 |

The 3 cells that c* loses are all genre cells. Its 3 style wins stay under both texts.
Late fusion gets 4 here against 5 on all held ArtELingo paintings, since one thin cell is a tie on this smaller set. No verdict changes.

## Glossary

- **c\*:** our frozen model. Full report: c*.
- **Plain Qwen:** the Qwen embedding of the text or image with no instruction.
- **Teacher's text:** the Qwen embedding of a text, given an instruction for one aspect.
- **Late fusion:** a weighted sum of c* and plain Qwen scores.
- **Cell won (WIN in the full report):** the interval of the difference is above 0, with 9 cells per test.
- **Shared paintings:** the 11,528 paintings in both held ArtELingo and MMArt.
- **New paintings:** the last test's 16,384 MMArt paintings outside ArtELingo.
