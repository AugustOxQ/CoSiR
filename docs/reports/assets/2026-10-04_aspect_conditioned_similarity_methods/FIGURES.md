# Figures for the stage report "aspect-conditioned similarity methods" (2026-10-02 to 2026-10-04)

Build: `prep_figure_data.py` reads the per-anchor npz files and result jsons and writes `figure_data.json` (one entry per plotted value with source file, key, seed, note); `make_figures.py` reads only that json. Pooled value = mean x 100 (pp); either = mean(r1 + other) x 100; R@1 = (either + gain) / 2 (asserted for every point). 95% CIs are cluster bootstraps (`src.eval.aspect_metrics`, clusters = painting groups). All values are seed-42 development episodes (12,288, 4,096 per pair) unless stated.

**task_schematic.png.** One aspect episode as text boxes: a query image with values a0 (aspect A) and b0 (aspect B), 4 support pairs for A (teal, four different A values, never a0), 4 contrast pairs for B (orange), 13 candidate captions (p_A teal, p_B orange, 11 grey negatives), and the swap test (swap supports and contrasts, p_B must move to the top). No data.

**chain.png.** The nine steps of the three days, one box each, with the headline numbers given in the brief (not recomputed; the ones that exist in the sources were cross-checked: RCA 13.38 / 0.10, cosine 12.96, A prime 16.52 vs 16.55, D0 hard 13.33 and told 21.06, N6 term 4.41, N6 nested +0.23 [-0.01, 0.47], N6c +0.15 [-0.06, 0.38], N1 -1.15). Grey = context or baseline, orange = failed its GO or gate, teal = new positive finding. Step 5 (MLLM) and step 9 (N6c) are coloured orange because their pre-registered rule or gate was not met.

**frontier.png.** Condition gain (y) against either rate (x), one point per scorer, seed 42. Left: full range with the zoom area dashed (D0 told at 40.27, 21.06 needs it). Right: zoom (either 20 to 38.6, gain -0.55 to 4.7), which is the chosen presentation: a two-panel full range plus zoom, since a broken axis would hide the empty space between the groups. Colour = family; circle = condition-aware score, square = condition-free control; hollow = label diagnostics and MLLM (Qwen3-VL-8B, seed 46, other episodes; cosine on those episodes has either 26.28). Dotted lines are iso-R@1 (gain = 2R - either) at 12.96 (cosine), 16.55 (E3 control, A prime control on seed 42) and 18.34 (C2, best condition-free). 14 points are named; the rest are numbered, with the key at the right (the dense groups use leader lines to a row below the axis). The Qwen3-VL-2B probe has no absolute either rate in its report and is omitted here. E1 C0 and SE are in the gain chart only. diag_relu and bilinear coincide to two decimals (points 2 and 3).

**gain_progression.png.** Condition gain with 95% CI, grouped: raw baselines (Wang 0.36, probe 0.35, the two best E1 by gain), factor rules (E1 C0 and SE; A3 agreement term and A3 N1 term, each scored alone), label-trained factors L3 (hatched, diagnostic), MLLMs (2B seed 44, 8B seed 46, different episodes, CIs from the reports), partition heads N6 T6 and T6soft (term only), label probes D0 hard and D0 told (hatched, diagnostic).

**margins.png.** Left: R@1 of each fused method minus its condition-free control (pp, 95% CI). Right: condition gain minus the same control (controls have gain 0, so it equals the method's gain). Rows: A prime nested A3 vs its control; N1 nested A3 vs declared control and vs matched control (seed 42); N1 nested A3 on fresh seeds 45, 47, 48 pooled (36,864 episodes; values from `addendum1.json` test.pooled.vs); N6 nested vs its control; N6c vs C1 (declared control) and C2 (matched control). Circles = declared control, squares = matched control.

**per_pair.png.** Condition gain (pp) by aspect pair (pair_index 0 emotion x style, 1 emotion x genre, 2 style x genre, 4,096 episodes each) for A3 agreement term, A3 N1 term, D0 hard, D0 told, N6 T6 term, N6 nested and N6c; cell values annotated. The D0 rows are label diagnostics.
