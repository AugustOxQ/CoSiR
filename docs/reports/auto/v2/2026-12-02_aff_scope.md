## Results brief

**What we tried.** We ran AFF (affect steering, our current best method) as a fixed recipe on two more frozen backbones, Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B and SigLIP 2, on ArtELingo's held paintings (reads H6, H7). Then we ran the language check on all three backbones: ArtELingo-28, 746 paintings with captions from 27 other languages, machine-translated to English (H8 CLIP, H9 Qwen, H10 SigLIP 2). [Spec](../../../superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-r9-aff-scope-design.md).

**Outcome against the bar.** Three of the five reads give a claim. R@1 points; B′(A0) is the condition-free scorer built on the reader's three groupings, the rule's main bar.
- H6 Qwen and H7 SigLIP 2: GO, all seven checks pass. AFF minus B′(A0): +0.63 [+0.49, +0.76] and +0.51 [+0.36, +0.66] (CLIP in round 6: +0.60).
- H9 Qwen on ArtELingo-28: full claim, both levels pass. AFF minus B′(A0): +0.37 [+0.23, +0.51]. Flagged languages: Emakhuwa, IsiNdebele; none set aside.
- H8 CLIP and H10 SigLIP 2 on ArtELingo-28: NO-GO (inconclusive). The condition gain over the matched control passes (+3.23 and +3.51). AFF minus B′(A0) is +0.06 and +0.15; the test cannot tell these from zero. The smallest margins it could detect were 0.21 and 0.26. Level 2 was not tested.

**What it does not show.**
- AFF is not shown to beat B′(A1), the strongest condition-free scorer. Descriptively, AFF minus B′(A1) is −0.52 to +0.13 over the five reads; its 95% interval lies below zero on H6, H8 and H9 (round 6 on CLIP: +0.14, inconclusive).
- No claim per aspect pair. Descriptively, AFF is below B′(A0) on style × genre in every read (−0.49 to −0.87).
- The claims hold on ArtELingo's held paintings (H6, H7) and the ArtELingo-28 read set (H9), pooled over three aspect pairs. No claim for a margin size, other datasets, native-language text, or any single language.
- H8 and H10 do not show that AFF fails on translated captions. They show that the test could not decide.
- ArtELingo's held paintings were read before (H1 to H5): all of H6's and H7's, and 248 of the 746 ArtELingo-28 paintings. H6 and H7 reuse H5's episodes. At least 2 of the 498 new paintings are the same artwork as a training painting, below the near-duplicate cut; none was excluded. All are disclosed.
- The SigLIP 2 results rest on one agent default (night mode, no answer possible). Its caption grouping gave a 1-row group, so the recipe refit it with the next seed. Your other option reports "the recipe does not run on SigLIP 2" and drops H7 and H10. The other agent defaults (Deviations) touch no verdict.
