# Buddy topic formation: next ArtELingo tests and a RedCaps transfer pilot

**Research memo, 27 September 2026.** This is a ranked experiment proposal; no experiment was run. “Value/cost” below means expected decision value or usable topic improvement divided by implementation **and verification** effort, not predicted metric gain. The counts are **six ArtELingo candidates (A1–A6)** and **three RedCaps candidates (B1–B3)**.

## What the evidence permits

The current ArtELingo reference is the two-teacher Attention-h1 student: a 50-D, train-fitted PCA content input and 28 GoEmotions caption probabilities project into a 32-D, unit-normalized one-head attention embedding. The content teacher is the CLIP image/text mutual-kNN buddy union; the affect teacher is a separate kNN graph over the 28 probabilities. Each supplies a symmetric InfoNCE loss. A binary graph of the final train embeddings is partitioned by Leiden **modularity** with its present default settings; held-out points receive the frozen train community vocabulary by cosine k=20 majority-vote transfer. Emotion-majority and genre labels are *evaluation only*. The source is `run_learned_student_arch_sweep_pilot.py`, `run_heldout_label_transfer_pilot.py`, `run_pipeline.py`, and `src/conditional_buddy/prototype_seed.py`.

The four-seed Attention-h1 baseline has held-out mean emotion/genre AMI **0.1241/0.2406**, mean silhouette **0.0397**, and **3/4** seeds clear the stated AMI Pareto bar. Cosine LR scheduling improves silhouette to **0.0466** in all four paired seeds without changing the 3/4 clearance. Schedule plus Leiden pseudo-contrastive training raises genre AMI to **0.3011** and silhouette to **0.0739**, but emotion AMI falls to **0.1160** and **0/4** seeds clear. Independent held-out Leiden was superseded for deployment by k=20 transfer, which improved the seed-42 AMIs from **0.1249/0.2404** to **0.1364/0.2530**. The matched audit found PercepT's silhouette lead real (**0.4973 versus 0.0416**) but its held-out center occupancy severely collapsed (**21/67 empty versus buddy's 0/19**). The subsequent image-only probe found no clean downstream buddy win: PercepT retained slightly more genre signal (**0.2973 versus 0.2625** AMI), while emotion was effectively tied. Buddy's actual Stage 2 mapper nevertheless reaches **0.5978** macro topic AUC versus its 0.5000 marginal baseline. These are different diagnostics, and a candidate must state which one it hopes to improve. Sources: `docs/reports/auto/percept/2026-09-26_artelingo_buddy_vs_percept_stage1.md` §§2–6a, `buddy_percept_downstream_probe_pilot_report.md`, and `buddy_stage2_pilot_report.md`.

The previous five brainstorm candidates were **all run**: matched audit/downstream probe, frozen-buddy-embedding input to PercepT, shared-width sweep, reconstruction-anchored DEC head, and soft Stage 2 targets. The six DEC-hybrid attempts collectively failed; wider 64/128-D shared spaces traded emotion and separation away for genre; soft targets tied or lost to hard targets. None is a new candidate here. See `docs/reports/auto/percept/2026-09-26_buddy_silhouette_gap_brainstorm.md` and the consolidated report §6.

## Part A — ArtELingo candidates

### A1. Sweep community granularity on the frozen embedding

**Mechanism and reason.** Repartition the **same** train student-embedding graph at several resolutions, then transfer held-out labels with the existing k=20 routine. The current `detect_communities` calls `leidenalg.ModularityVertexPartition` without a resolution parameter; a real sweep must use a resolution-aware Leiden objective (for example RBConfiguration at gamma values around 1) and first check that gamma=1 reproduces the existing modularity partition closely enough to be a fair anchor. The current 19 balanced train communities and the PercepT occupancy/silhouette split show that cluster granularity could change the apparent geometry and downstream utility even with an unchanged encoder. No resolution sweep is reported in the consolidated investigation.

**Smallest test.** On the seed-42 frozen train/held-out embedding snapshot, predeclare a small gamma grid (for example 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2); record community count, occupancy, stability to Leiden seed, train-vocabulary coverage after k=20 transfer, both held-out AMIs, and the matched-protocol silhouette. Retrain the Stage 2 mapper/probe only for the best *prespecified, noncollapsed* one or two partitions, with the official held-out labels reserved for reporting rather than repeated selection. **Cost/risk:** low-to-medium CPU work, then medium mapper work if a partition survives; the main risk is optimizing AMI on the held-out set or mistaking a changed number of topics for better topics.

### A2. Sweep ArtELingo graph K, with an alpha sanity check

**Mechanism and reason.** `run_pipeline.py` chose **K=20** for ~61,402 paintings as an explicit judgment call, not a validated ArtELingo optimum. Experiment 16's RedCaps 150k/300k/500k K(N) sweep found K can change strict/union composition and retrieval; it does **not** establish a transferable K for a smaller, differently sampled art corpus, nor for emotion/genre topic quality. Rebuild only the ArtELingo **content** teacher at K∈{10,20,30,50}, keeping affect K=20 and all training settings fixed, so the first contrast isolates content topology. Log `img_only`/`txt_only`/`both`/`repair` fractions, degree, disconnected components, and teacher/student graph recall before interpreting AMI changes. A later paired check can vary affect K separately.

**Smallest test.** One paired seed-42 screen against K=20, then the three other existing seeds for any promising K; measure k=20 transferred held-out AMIs, occupancy, and matched silhouette, and preserve the Stage 2/probe gate before adopting it. **Alpha is not an independent useful binary-graph sweep here:** `build_buddy_graphs` forms an unweighted union; `alpha=0.5` only selects MST bridges in `ensure_connected` when components remain. First count those bridges. Only propose alpha as a real knob if the experiment explicitly changes edge weights or bridge construction and states the new graph objective. **Cost/risk:** medium graph rebuilding and four-seed verification; main risk is shifting both teacher loss and Leiden topology accidentally, or treating RedCaps' K=39-at-500k retrieval finding as an ArtELingo prediction.

### A3. Consensus across seed partitions, without averaging unaligned vectors

**Mechanism and reason.** Baseline Pareto clearance is 3/4, and the schedule gives only a modest separation gain. A consensus could reduce seed-specific boundary assignments without selecting the best held-out seed. Raw 32-D vectors from separately trained students cannot safely be averaged: contrastive solutions may be rotated, and Leiden IDs have no common meaning. Instead, align paintings by ID; score candidate edges by the fraction of the four train partitions that coassign their endpoints; partition that sparse consensus graph once; transfer the resulting fixed vocabulary to held-out embeddings by a defined ensemble vote or a reference seed's embedding. Keep baseline/schedule variants separate in the first pass so the pseudo-contrastive emotion trade-off is not hidden inside a consensus.

**Smallest test.** Audit whether all four per-seed embeddings/partitions were saved (the visible standalone snapshot is seed 42); rerun/save only missing seeds if needed. Compare seed-42, four-seed consensus, and the mean/range of individual seeds on AMIs, occupancy, stability, and the same image-only genre/emotion probe. **Cost/risk:** medium because artifacts may need regeneration and held-out assignment for a consensus vocabulary needs validation; risk is correlated errors or a consensus that erases rare affect structure.

### A4. Control edge-type contribution to the content teacher

**Mechanism and reason.** The buddy union's image-only, text-only, corroborated (`both`), and repair edges are not equally strong evidence, yet `upper_triangle_edges` samples positives uniformly. RedCaps Experiment 16 found `both` edges only **1.2–2.8%** of the union across its K grid and many strict-isolated nodes; that composition may differ on ArtELingo, so measure it first rather than importing a RedCaps weight. A modest provenance-aware positive-pair sampler could upweight `both` edges and downweight repair edges while preserving union coverage; changing K and provenance weights in one pilot would confound the result.

**Smallest test.** On the existing K=20 ArtELingo graph, tabulate edge types and repair/bridge counts; then compare natural uniform sampling with one fixed modest `both` upweight and repair exclusion at identical positive-pair budget, seed 42 first and four seeds only if the held-out AMI pair and downstream probe improve. **Cost/risk:** medium-to-high; sparse `both` coverage may concentrate gradients on a tiny, nonrepresentative subset. Experiment 16's flat **22.7–23.0×** RedCaps subreddit lift across K argues against assuming more `both` edges automatically improve topic quality.

### A5. Gate Stage 2 depth on a measured capacity gap

**Mechanism and reason.** The actual mapper has **one learned query**, scaled dot-product pooling over 50 frozen 512-D CLIP patches, then a single linear topic head; no deeper head has been tested. Its training BCE declines from **0.5188 at epoch 10** to **0.2003 at epoch 100**; the last ten epochs still fall from **0.2030**, but there is no train-versus-validation loss or train AUC curve in that report. Thus the curve does **not** establish underfitting. Macro held-out topic AUC **0.5978**, with several topics below 0.5, can also reflect noisy targets, rare communities, or visual ambiguity. Widening the Stage 1 student already worsened emotion and silhouette; that is no evidence for deeper Stage 2.

**Smallest test.** First rerun the unchanged mapper and log train/validation per-topic AUC and class frequency on a split of train paintings. Only if both losses/AUCs indicate a capacity ceiling, compare one small post-pooling nonlinear head (for example 512→256→19 with regularization) at the same optimization budget, then use the *common external* emotion/genre probe plus raw mean-patch control as the deciding outcome. **Cost/risk:** medium; patch cache exists for ArtELingo, but more capacity may memorize train labels and improve topic AUC while losing human-label signal.

### A6. Audit a genuinely additional teacher view before training one

**Mechanism and reason.** The pseudo-contrastive variant's **genre gain/ emotion loss** makes a new view plausible only if it supplies information beyond the present content and GoEmotions graphs. Genre in `run_pipeline.py` comes from a separate `genre_map` with only **1,144 train-overlap** and **159 held-out** labeled paintings; using it as a teacher would turn the method partly supervised and invalidate the same genre AMI as an independent evaluation. Art-historical style/period metadata is not established as available in the required files. Caption sentiment beyond GoEmotions might be cheap, but is likely correlated with the existing 28 probabilities; CLIP caption semantics are already in the content teacher.

**Smallest test.** Audit coverage and provenance of possible style/period fields, and compute candidate-view neighbor overlap and conditional association with evaluation labels *after* the existing content/affect views. If one view is both train-available and complementary, try a third graph loss at a fixed small weight, report the six original seed/variant comparisons, and reserve an independent external outcome for evaluation. **Cost/risk:** high relative to current evidence: missing metadata, label leakage, noisy proxy sentiment, and another genre-over-emotion trade-off.

## Part B — RedCaps without emotion or genre supervision

### Exact transfer boundary

**Available unchanged:** the CLIP image/text mutual-kNN graph primitives (`mutual_knn`, `union_graph`, degree/component repairs) and its edge provenance, symmetric InfoNCE on positive pairs, Leiden on a binary student graph, cosine k=20 train-vocabulary transfer, and the single-query image-patch mapper concept. These are algorithms, not a promise that the same hyperparameters or code paths scale unchanged. RedCaps' validated subreddit-lift evaluator is `redcaps_buddy.subreddit_lift(data, upper_triangle_edges, top_k=None)`: it compares the fraction of same-subreddit edges with the expectation from the graph's own **edge-endpoint subreddit marginals**. Reuse this function and its reliability filter; do not substitute raw same-subreddit purity or per-subreddit lift alone. Experiment 9's analysis warns that per-subreddit lift has a strong degree/size normalization effect. The RedCaps 150k/300k-diverse/500k-diverse stores exist; the plain 300k/500k stores are subreddit-grouped prefixes and cannot be exchanged for the `_diverse` stores in an evaluation comparison. Sources: `buddy_graph.py`, `analyze_subreddit_correlates.py`, publication-plan §4 Experiment 16, and both September K reports.

**Must change:** ArtELingo's 28-D GoEmotions affect probabilities and affect-neighbor graph do not exist as an established RedCaps signal; the 50-D PCA content input is fit for paintings and must be refit on RedCaps train data; the two-input projection currently expects content/affect dimensions (50/28); and `run_pipeline.py`'s painting deduplication, multilingual emotion majorities, `genre_map`, Pareto bar, and AMI reporting are ArtELingo-specific. RedCaps is one image per caption and exposes subreddit via image path only as an *evaluation proxy*, not a teacher, loss, split rule, or hyperparameter-selection label. Stage 2's `[N,50,512]` ArtELingo patch cache and fixed **19**-topic head do not transfer; the head width must equal the discovered RedCaps train community count. The existing RedCaps feature stores contain global 512-D image/text features, not an established patch-token cache. Finally, ArtELingo's `run_learned_student_arch_sweep_pilot.py` computes one affect loss from a full train forward pass; copying that literally at 150k–500k is an unnecessary memory/compute risk. Sample/reindex both graph-positive batches when implementing the RedCaps pilot.

### B1. First transfer test: one content teacher, then Leiden (recommended first tonight)

**Teacher and student.** Keep the existing RedCaps CLIP image/text mutual-kNN **union graph** as the sole teacher; start at K=30, the validated 150k anchor, and record raw-union versus repaired-edge counts. A single content teacher is a deliberate hypothesis: the ArtELingo affect loss supplies a *different target*, while RedCaps has no established affect target. Creating an emotion pseudo-teacher from captions would import an unvalidated task bias. For the smallest Attention-h1-family student, project train-fitted image and text features to two 32-D tokens, keep one-head attention plus LayerNorm/L2 output, but optimize **one** symmetric InfoNCE loss on sampled union edges. This keeps attention nondegenerate without falsely treating the two input modalities as two independent teacher graphs. Compare an equally sized simple concat/mean projection as an architecture sanity control. Partition the train student graph by the existing default Leiden; assign held-out points to that *frozen* train vocabulary by cosine k=20, never by independently numbered held-out Leiden labels. The number of discovered communities and class occupancy are outcomes, not preselected ArtELingo constants.

**Smallest cheap-first experiment.** Use only `redcaps_150k`, split by stable sample ID into roughly 120k train, 15k validation, 15k final test without consulting subreddit. Build the existing K=30 teacher graph **on train only**; retain a graph-only Leiden/transfer baseline. Train one local-GPU seed with the same sampled-pair batch size and at most 200 epochs/recall-plateau rule, using sampled-node forwards; form student kNN graphs and compute the exact `subreddit_lift` function on **validation** edges for student and matched raw-feature/teacher controls at the same validation N and K. Report overall lift, observed and expected same-subreddit fractions, qualifying subreddits, edge types, coverage/occupancy, and seed-42 result; no subreddit enters optimization. A strict claim needs at least three paired seeds and a sealed final test split. Graph building at this scale is measured in seconds-to-minutes on the existing exact GPU path; with CPU Leiden and student checkpoints, budget **roughly 15–45 minutes on one local GPU for a one-seed pilot**, explicitly an estimate because this RedCaps student has not been benchmarked. No patch extraction and no DAS6 are needed for this first gate. A null lift gain is informative but does not by itself rule out a better downstream topic bottleneck; proceed to B2 only if partitions are populated and the student preserves meaningful validation lift relative to the graph-only/raw controls. **Cost/risk:** medium implementation, low compute, medium verification; the chief risk is learning a cosmetically smooth copy of the input union graph rather than useful image-predictable topics.

### B2. RedCaps-native Stage 2 and the decisive subreddit probe

**Mechanism and evaluation.** Freeze B1's train communities and k=20 held-out transfer targets. Train an image-only mapper from frozen CLIP **patch tokens** to train topic IDs, with the ArtELingo single-query pooling architecture and a head sized to the actual RedCaps vocabulary. Because these are hard, mutually exclusive Leiden labels, use the matched downstream-probe pilot's single-label **cross-entropy** and softmax convention; report per-topic AUC against a train-marginal baseline as a mapper diagnostic, not as a cross-system quality verdict. The deciding evaluation mirrors `buddy_percept_downstream_probe_pilot_report.md`: fit the same regularized logistic probe from **validation** predicted topic softmax scores to **subreddit**, then score it on the sealed test images; compare its AMI/accuracy with a probe from raw mean-pooled patch features fitted and scored on those identical splits. Explicitly report topic-minus-raw differences, including whether either metric actually beats the raw control, plus majority-class accuracy and per-class support. Use only subreddits with enough examples for the declared stratified evaluation, and report retained coverage. Subreddit is noisy topic-adjacent metadata, so a small gap is not proof of semantic superiority. If raw features win as on ArtELingo, also report the fraction of raw signal retained by the topic bottleneck rather than describing it as a win. The graph-only B1 topics should receive the same mapper/probe budget so the student has a fair control.

**Smallest test and gate.** First verify image access and patch-token extraction for the 150k records; the existing global-feature store does not itself supply `[N,50,512]` tokens. At float32, that cache is roughly **15 GB for 150k images**, before overhead; use a limited validation pilot if storage or extraction dominates. Fit one mapper for the B1 seed and the graph-only baseline, then repeat seeds only for a real probe gain. This is **medium-to-high cost** and may take substantially longer than B1 because images must be re-encoded; do not spend DAS6/300k/500k resources until B1's cheap gate and this 150k downstream comparison justify them.

### B3. Add a second teacher only after proving it is independent and helpful

**Mechanism and reason.** A second loss built from the *same CLIP image-only or text-only mutual graph* merely repackages edges already in the union; it is an edge-composition ablation, not an affect-teacher analogue. Subreddit must remain evaluation-only. If B1 loses lift or B2's probe exposes a useful missing view, audit an actually different representation already available or cheaply extractable, such as a DINOv2 visual or independent caption encoder graph, for held-out neighbor complementarity and subreddit lift. Only then compare union-only InfoNCE with one additional graph loss at fixed total positive-pair/gradient budget. Preserve a one-teacher control at every seed, since the ArtELingo two-teacher result does not establish that two losses are intrinsically better. **Smallest test:** graph overlap/lift audit on 150k, then one seed and a three-seed follow-up only for a populated-community and downstream-probe improvement. **Cost/risk:** high feature/extraction and confounding cost; a second content view may increase genre-like content signal while reducing other structure, just as ArtELingo's pseudo-contrastive variant traded away emotion.

**First implementation tonight: B1, one local-GPU RedCaps-150k seed plus its graph-only baseline.** It is the cheapest test of the most consequential unknown: whether the content-graph student produces stable, populated, held-out subreddit-enriched communities when no affect teacher exists. Keep the 15k final test split sealed; a positive validation pilot earns three seeds and B2, while a null result redirects attention to teacher/architecture diagnosis before spending on patch extraction or DAS6. In parallel, A1 is the shortest ArtELingo-only experiment if the immediate objective is improving the already-working art pipeline rather than establishing transfer. Nothing here justifies 300k/500k topic-pipeline training tonight.

## Open design points and brief ambiguity

1. **Which graph defines the published subreddit-lift anchor?** `analyze_subreddit_correlates.py` scores the un-repaired `union_graph(A_img,A_txt)`; Experiment 16 Stage A scores the degree-repaired union. Both call the same `subreddit_lift` formula. The brief says “reuse it exactly” and “content teacher unchanged” without choosing the graph treatment. Report lift on **both**, label each graph explicitly, and use the *actual training graph* as the matched student control. Never compare one arm's repaired lift with another arm's raw-union lift as if they were identical protocols.
2. **RedCaps patch availability and image-access cost are unverified.** The named stores document global 512-D features. B2 must confirm image paths, token extraction, and storage before promising an ArtELingo-shaped patch mapper or a wall-clock estimate.
3. **The single-teacher input form is a genuine design choice.** Two CLIP tokens with one union loss preserve nontrivial Attention-h1 fusion; a single concatenated content token would make self-attention degenerate. B1's cheap projection control tests whether attention helps before treating its presence as essential.
4. **Resolution is an objective change, not a hidden existing flag.** Current Leiden modularity has no exposed gamma; compare the gamma=1 resolution-aware partition with the current one before interpreting a sweep.
