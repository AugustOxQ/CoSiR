# D2 ablation: the instructed graphs carry most of the gain; without the caption terms, style AMI rises

> 2026-10-21 (sequence name 12-22) · Spec: [design](../../../superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-d2-ablation-design.md) · Decision rule: none (descriptive) · Run log: the round folder [src/test/20261021_d2_ablation/](../../../../src/test/20261021_d2_ablation/).

## Results brief

**What we tried.** We changed one part of our model (D2) at a time: 12 variants, 3 seeds each, on 5,000 development
paintings ([spec](../../../superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-d2-ablation-design.md)). We also compared the four Qwen image
embeddings (default; emotion, style and genre instructions). AMI: agreement of k-means clusters (k = 20) with labels.

**Outcome against the bar.** No bar: the work is descriptive. Effect = the variant's image AMI minus our model's
(0.0962 emotion, 0.3697 style, 0.4540 genre); the words are defined in Results.

| Change | Emotion | Style | Genre |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| One default-view graph | −0.0222, clear | −0.1681, clear | −0.0287, clear |
| No buddy terms | −0.0214, clear | −0.1473, clear | −0.0449, clear |
| No teacher | −0.0032, not resolved | −0.0106, small | +0.0063, not resolved |
| Default-view teacher | −0.0041, not resolved | −0.0048, not resolved | +0.0077, not resolved |
| Image endpoints only | +0.0057, small | +0.0248, clear | −0.0086, not resolved |

- No instruction anywhere: 0.0733 / 0.1848 / 0.4241, below PercepT core (0.0772 / 0.2286 / 0.4359).
- Image endpoints only reaches style 0.3945; the instructed Qwen image has 0.3952.
- Instructed inputs leave genre at 0.4545, below their own input (0.4956).

**What it does not show.** Development data only: these paintings chose our model, so removals may look worse, and
the variants that beat it were picked on them too. Without the caption terms the teacher's share of the loss rises
from 1/5 to 1/3; we cannot separate the two. Three seeds; no held data; one dataset and backbone. Amendment A1, a
night agent default, needs your check (Deviations).

<summary>Our reading and recommendation (open after writing yours)</summary>

**Our reading.**
- The instructed graphs are the core of the method: their pairs, their structure and their instruction each cost
  clearly on all three aspects. The instruction acts through the graphs, not the teacher (spec §6).
- The teacher is close to redundant beside the graphs. Alone it lifts style from 0.2217 (default image) to 0.2702.
- Without the caption terms, emotion and style rise; two variants agree. The cause is open: fewer caption terms or a
  larger teacher share (with image endpoints only, CKA with the instructed style image rises from 0.823 to 0.877). Image and caption also agree
  less under the emotion and style instructions (CKA 0.521, 0.450 against 0.618), not checked as a cause.
- "Any backbone" needs Qwen's instructed graphs at training: the default-view graph variant (best epochs 2 to 4) is
  also below PercepT core on all three aspects.
- Genre is the open problem, and the input is not its limit.
- How sure: fairly sure of the graph and teacher readings; the caption reading is a lead.

**Our recommendation.** Option 1 first: cheap, and it fixes the recipe that options 2 and 3 build on. If retrieval
needs captions, prefer no own-caption terms: it keeps the caption network and raises its AMI on emotion and style
(0.1983 and 0.1793 against 0.1828 and 0.1728).

## Results

Every statement in this report is an attribution on development data: 5,000 evaluation paintings (genre 4,053), three seeds, one
dataset and backbone, no held data. The seed range is the full model's maximum minus minimum over its three seeds
(table below). Effect words, used the same way throughout:

- **Not resolved:** the 95% interval includes 0, or the effect is at most one seed range. This is not "no effect".
- **Small:** the interval excludes 0 and the effect is between one and two seed ranges.
- **Clear:** the interval excludes 0 and the effect is over two seed ranges.

The 5,000 paintings chose the full model, so removals may look worse than they are. A row that beats it (such as
image endpoints only) is also picked on these paintings: read it as a lead, not as a better model. Image endpoints
only, no own-caption terms and no buddy terms also give the teacher 1/3 of the loss weight, against 1/5 in the full
model (loss = buddy + own-caption + 0.5 teacher); image endpoints only also gives the image–image buddy term 2/3,
against 1/10. This ablation does not separate those weights from the removal.

## Analysis

Effect words follow the definition under Results. Removal effects need not add. Not every row is one change: no
instruction anywhere combines one default-view graph and no teacher; distillation only and image endpoints only
remove groups of terms.

- **Emotion.** Clear costs: no buddy terms, no graph terms (distillation only), one default-view graph, random pairs,
  no instruction anywhere. Small cost: no content correction. Small gains: no own-caption terms, image endpoints
  only. Not resolved: separate heads, no teacher, default-view teacher, instructed inputs.
- **Style.** Clear costs: no buddy terms, no graph terms, no content correction, one default-view graph, random pairs,
  no instruction anywhere. Clear gains: no own-caption terms, image endpoints only. Small: no teacher (cost),
  instructed inputs (gain). Not resolved: separate heads, default-view teacher.
- **Genre.** Clear costs: no buddy terms, one default-view graph, random pairs, no instruction anywhere. Small cost:
  no graph terms. Not resolved: separate heads, no teacher, default-view teacher, no own-caption terms, image
  endpoints only, no content correction, instructed inputs.

Image endpoints only on style (0.3945) is close to the instructed Qwen image (0.3952); no test of that difference.

**Our reading:** In these data the instruction acts mainly through the graphs: one default-view graph has a clear cost on
all three aspects. The default-view teacher (the student's own default input, same weight and temperature; spec D17)
is not resolved on any aspect, and dropping the teacher costs style a small amount.

**Our reading:** No instruction anywhere is below PercepT core on all three aspects, and so is one default-view graph
with the teacher kept (best epochs 2 to 4), so the epoch-one caveat does not carry the whole reading. No test of these
differences was run; they license no "any backbone" claim.

**Our reading:** Instructed inputs raise style by a small amount and leave emotion and genre not resolved. They stay
below the instructed image on style (0.3857 against 0.3952) and genre (0.4545 against 0.4956), above it on emotion
(0.0993 against 0.0929). For genre, the input is not the limit.

**Our reading:** Diagnostics point to cluster geometry. Style alignment with the instructed image:

|---|---|---|

|Default graph|0.601|0.319|

These metrics diagnose geometry, not an information bound.

Style probes may read low: 5/5 folds unconverged for image endpoints (all seeds), no own-caption terms (seeds 0/1);
instructed inputs: 0/1/3. AMI is unaffected. All other rows: zero.

Entropy is high (0.981–0.995 over all rows): no single-cluster collapse. Balanced sizes do not imply useful classes.
